The compressed excerpts in `paperforge/modules/case-library/assets/study-figures/` accompany paper-specific analysis. They remain attributable to the respective paper authors and rights holders. They are not original output figures or reusable experiment results for a new paper.

Paper titles, source links, versions and figure context are recorded in:

- [Core paper cards and figure commentary](paperforge/modules/case-library/references/paper-cards.md)
- [Additional first/co-first paper cards](paperforge/modules/case-library/references/core-cards-2025-2026.md)

## Conference metadata and analyses

Official-source provenance, snapshot dates and retrieval status appear in the [conference manifest](paperforge/modules/top-ml/references/corpus/manifest.json) and [evidence base](paperforge/modules/top-ml/references/evidence-base.md). Metadata records and sampled structural analyses do not imply that all full papers were read or that their conclusions were verified.

No full paper PDFs or author-owned editable drawing projects are included.
