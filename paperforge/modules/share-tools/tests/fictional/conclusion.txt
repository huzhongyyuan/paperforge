In this paper, we present TideMotion, a frequency-aware framework for video motion transfer that separates motion features by band and adapts each band independently. To remove the competition between fine motion and global structure, we design a Frequency-routed Motion Prior. We further introduce Band-wise Test-time Adaptation, which tunes only the experts relevant to a given reference video, and a Spectral Alignment Loss, which aligns motion independently of appearance.
